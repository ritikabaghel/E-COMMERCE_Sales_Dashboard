# E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

## 📌 Project Goal

The goal of this project is to analyze e-commerce sales data and provide actionable insights into revenue, profit, customer behavior, and product performance. The dashboard helps track business performance across multiple dimensions such as category, payment mode, location, and customer contribution.

---

## 📊 Key Insights

* Total Profit: **37K** | Total Sales Amount: **438K** | Total Quantity Sold: **5615**
* **Clothing (63%)** drives the majority of sales, followed by Electronics and Furniture.
* **Cash on Delivery (44%)** is the most preferred payment method, with UPI and cards contributing significantly.
* **Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra** generated the highest profit, while certain months recorded losses.
* Top customers like **Madhav** and **Madan** contribute significantly to revenue.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** → Dashboard creation, visualization, and interactive analysis
* **Excel** → Data cleaning, preprocessing, and aggregation

---

## 📂 Features of the Dashboard

* **KPI Cards**: Total Sales, Profit, Quantity, and AOV (Average Order Value)
* **Trend Analysis**: Monthly profit fluctuations
* **Customer Analysis**: Top contributing customers by sales amount
* **Category Insights**: Profit and sales by product category and sub-category
* **Payment Mode Analysis**: Breakdown of revenue by payment methods
* **Geographical Analysis**: State-wise profit contribution

---

## 🎯 Purpose of the Project

* Enable data-driven decision-making for e-commerce businesses
* Identify profitable product categories, customers, and regions
* Track business health using KPIs and monthly performance trends
* Support strategy for improving revenue and reducing losses

---

## 📌 How This Helps

This dashboard provides a **comprehensive view of sales performance**, allowing business owners and analysts to:

* Spot profitable products and regions
* Optimize marketing and sales strategies
* Understand customer preferences in payment modes
* Monitor seasonal/monthly profit fluctuations
